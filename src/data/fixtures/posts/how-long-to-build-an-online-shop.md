"How long will it take?" is the second question every client asks, right after the price. The answer depends less on the code than most people expect.

## How long does each type of shop take?

| Type            | Timeline        | What it usually includes                                             |
| --------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Catalogue site  | 2 to 3 weeks    | Products, prices, enquiry and WhatsApp buttons, no online payment    |
| Online shop     | 4 to 6 weeks    | Catalogue, cart, eSewa, Khalti and card payments, order emails       |
| Custom platform | 8 weeks or more | Customer accounts, coupons, integrations with delivery or accounting |

These timelines start when the written scope is signed, not at the first call.

## What happens week by week?

This is a typical six week shop project:

1. **Week 1: scope and content.** The scope is signed. You start collecting product photos and descriptions. We apply for payment merchant accounts with you.
2. **Week 2: design.** We design the home page, category page, product page and checkout, and share them on a preview link.
3. **Weeks 3 and 4: build.** The shop is built on the approved design. Payments are connected in test mode. Products start going in.
4. **Week 5: content and testing.** The full catalogue goes in. We test the checkout on real phones, on slow connections, and with every payment method.
5. **Week 6: training and launch.** Your team learns how to add products and manage orders. We launch and watch the first orders closely.

## What slows an online shop project down?

In our experience, delays rarely come from the code. They come from four places:

- **Product content.** Photos and descriptions for 200 products take longer than anyone expects. This is the most common delay by far.
- **Payment approvals.** Merchant accounts need documents and review time. A missing document can add a week.
- **Too many approvers.** When five people must agree on every design, each round takes days instead of hours.
- **Changes mid-build.** New features added after the scope is signed push the launch date. We write them down and schedule them for after launch where we can.

## How can I get my shop live sooner?

Do these before or during the first week:

- Put every product in a spreadsheet: name, price, variants, description and photo file names.
- Photograph products against a plain background in good daylight. Consistent photos matter more than expensive ones.
- Decide who approves designs, and keep it to one or two people.
- Gather the documents for payment providers: business registration, PAN, and bank account details.

## Should I launch with everything, or start small?

Start small. Launch with the products that sell best, one or two payment methods and the pages customers need to trust you. Add the rest in the weeks after launch, when real orders show you what matters.

A shop that launches in five weeks and improves every month beats a perfect shop that launches in five months.
