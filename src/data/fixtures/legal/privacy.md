This policy explains what we collect when you use this website or contact us, and what we do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

## Who we are

Joonix Studio is a software development agency in Kathmandu, Nepal. When this policy says "we" or "us", it means Joonix Studio. You can reach us about anything in this policy at the email address in the footer of every page.

## What we collect

**When you send an enquiry**, we collect what you type into the form: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number if you give one, the service and budget you choose, and your message. We also record the page you sent it from and the time.

**When you browse the site**, our hosting provider records standard server logs: the page requested, the time, your IP address and your browser type. We use these logs to keep the site secure and working.

**On your device**, the site saves one setting in your browser's local storage: whether you chose the light or dark theme. It never leaves your device.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

## Why we collect it

- To reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to run your project.
- To stop spam and abuse of the contact form.
- To keep the website secure and fix problems.

## Who processes it for us

- **Amazon Web Services** hosts the website and stores enquiries in its Mumbai region (India).
- **Amazon Simple Email Service** sends the emails about your enquiry.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that the contact form is used by a person, not a bot. It may process your IP address and browser details for that check.

Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy terms and only for the service it provides to us.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries are deleted automatically 24 months after you send them, unless you become a client. Client records are kept for as long as Nepali law requires for business records. Server logs are deleted after 90 days.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. If you no longer want to hear from us, say so in any reply and we will stop.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of the page. If a change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will tell you by email.
